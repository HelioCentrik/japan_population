Metrics & Queries

How to derive demographic metrics from the census star schema powering the Japan Population Dashboard — a Dash/Plotly application visualizing 100 years of Japanese census data (1920–2020) across 47 prefectures, with IPSS projections through 2065. All queries run against v_census unless otherwise noted. Seven metrics are computed from first principles; none rely on pre-stored aggregates.

age_scheme = 'scheme_a'

Always use standard 5-year bands (0–4 through 85+) for derived metrics. scheme_b is a kazoedoshi offset present only in the 1945 data.

age_group != 'Total'

Never use the pre-stored Total row in arithmetic — it double-counts. Sum individual bands instead.

area_level = 2

Filter to prefecture level. Omitting this includes the national aggregate row (area_level = 1) and produces double-counted totals.

age_start >= 65

Safe 65+ filter pattern. Captures both closed and open-ended terminal bands regardless of which terminal band a given census year uses.

v_map_metrics

For choropleth map data: use this pre-aggregated view with deltas. Do not query v_census for map data.

sex = 'total'

For all aggregate metrics unless computing sex-disaggregated figures (e.g. the population pyramid or sex ratio).

Seven Metrics

Derived Metrics

1
Aging Index
高齢化指数
KPI card
Formula
Σ pop (65+) Σ pop (0–14) × 100

The headline metric. Captures Japan's demographic inversion from a child-heavy to an elderly-heavy society. The threshold at 100 is intuitive: below 100 means more children than elderly; above 100 means the elderly outnumber children. Japan crossed 100 for the first time in 2000.

National 14.4 (1920) → 13.9 (1950) → 66.2 (1990) → 119.1 (2000) ✕ 100 → ~211 (2015)
SQL v_census 
SELECT area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja,
    ROUND(
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 65 THEN population ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 /
        NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN age_start <= 10 AND age_end <= 14 THEN population ELSE 0 END), 0)
    , 1) AS aging_index
FROM v_census
WHERE year = {year}
  AND age_scheme = 'scheme_a'
  AND age_group != 'Total'
  AND sex = 'total'
  AND area_level = 2
GROUP BY area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja
ORDER BY aging_index DESC
2
Old-Age Dependency Ratio
老年従属人口指数
computed in view
Formula
Σ pop (65+) Σ pop (15–64) × 100

Quantifies the economic burden shift — how many elderly people exist per 100 working-age people. Crossed the youth dependency ratio between 1995 and 2000, marking a structural turning point. Available in v_map_metrics as old_age_dep but is not a selectable map metric; replaced by Population Change (pop_delta).

National ~8.6 (1920) → ~10.3 (1970) → 25.5 (2000) → ~43.5 (2015)
Note: Do not add old_age_dep back to MAP_METRICS in config.py. The design decision to replace it with pop_delta is intentional.
3
Youth Dependency Ratio
年少従属人口指数
Formula
Σ pop (0–14) Σ pop (15–64) × 100

The mirror of old-age dependency. Japan began as a high-youth-burden society in 1920 (~63.0), falling steadily as birth rates declined through the postwar decades. By 2000 it had inverted relative to old-age dependency — more economic burden from elderly than children for the first time in recorded history.

National ~63.0 (1920) → ~35.2 (1970) → 21.4 (2000) → ~20.6 (2015)
4
Total Dependency Ratio
従属人口指数
Formula
Σ pop(0–14) + Σ pop(65+) Σ pop (15–64) × 100

Traces a U-shaped arc across the century: high in 1920 (driven by youth), falling to a trough around 1990, then rising again driven by the elderly. The trough period (~1970–1995) is Japan's demographic dividend — maximum working-age share, minimum dependency burden. This window correlates directly with Japan's high-growth economic era.

Demographic dividend window: The trough at ~43.5 in 1990 represents peak productive capacity — the lowest total burden per working-age person in Japan's recorded history.
5
Working-Age Share
生産年齢人口割合
KPI card
Formula
Σ pop (15–64) Σ total pop × 100

Share of the population in the prime working-age band. Peaked at ~69.5% in 1990–1995 and has been declining since. A falling working-age share, combined with a rising elderly share, drives the structural pension and healthcare funding pressures Japan faces today.

National ~58% (1920) → ~69.5% (1990–1995) → declining (2000+)
6
Children 0–14 Share
年少人口割合
Formula
Σ pop (0–14) Σ total pop × 100

Share of the youngest cohort. Japan began as a demographically young nation — more than one in three people was under 15 in 1920. By 2015, that had fallen below one in eight. The 少子化 (declining birthrate) trend is visible in every census year from 1950 onward.

National ~36.5% (1920) → ~24.0% (1970) → ~14.6% (2000) → ~12.9% (2015)
7
Sex Ratio by Age Band
性比
Per age band
male pop female pop × 100

Computed per age band. The natural ratio is ~103 (slightly more males born than females). Significant deviation reveals historical events. The WWII male mortality notch is visible in the 25–29 band in 1950 (ratio: 83.8), then walks up the pyramid at 5 years per census — reaching 55–59 in 1975 (ratio: 79.7 — the dataset minimum).

WWII notch: The sex ratio anomaly is the most visually striking feature of the population pyramid. It migrates predictably: 25–29 (1950) → 30–34 (1955) → … → 55–59 (1975).
SQL v_census 
SELECT age_group, age_start,
    SUM(CASE WHEN sex = 'male'   THEN population END) AS male_pop,
    SUM(CASE WHEN sex = 'female' THEN population END) AS female_pop,
    ROUND(
        SUM(CASE WHEN sex = 'male' THEN population END) * 100.0 /
        NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN sex = 'female' THEN population END), 0)
    , 1) AS sex_ratio
FROM v_census
WHERE year = {year}
  AND age_scheme = 'scheme_a'
  AND age_group != 'Total'
  AND sex != 'total'
  AND area_level = 2
GROUP BY age_group, age_start
ORDER BY age_start
Pattern

Reusable Age-Bucket CTE

Base pattern for computing all five dependency and share metrics in a single pass. The CTE does one scan of the data; all derived columns are computed in the outer SELECT. Use this for KPI cards and time-series queries — it avoids redundant subqueries and keeps the ratio computations in one place.

pop_0_14

Children — numerator for youth dependency and children share. Uses age_end ≤ 14 filter to avoid the 10–14 band edge case.

pop_15_64

Working-age — denominator for all dependency ratios. Shared denominator across aging index, youth dep, old-age dep, and total dep.

pop_65_plus

Elderly — uses age_start ≥ 65 only (no age_end filter) to correctly capture open-ended terminal bands (80+, 85+) regardless of year.

pop_total

Denominator for share calculations. SUM of all individual bands, not the Total row — this excludes ~1.45M persons with unknown age in early census years.

aging_index

pop_65_plus / pop_0_14 × 100. Crosses 100 in 2000.

old_age_dep

pop_65_plus / pop_15_64 × 100. Not exposed as a map metric; computed for KPI and reference.

youth_dep

pop_0_14 / pop_15_64 × 100. Crossed below old_age_dep between 1995–2000.

working_age_share

pop_15_64 / pop_total × 100. Peaked ~1990; proxy for demographic dividend window.

SQL — Age Bucket CTE (all metrics, one pass) 
WITH age_buckets AS (
    SELECT year, area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 0  AND age_end <= 14 THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_0_14,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 15 AND age_end <= 64 THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_15_64,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 65                   THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_65_plus,
        SUM(population)                                                              AS pop_total
    FROM v_census
    WHERE age_scheme  = 'scheme_a'
      AND age_group  != 'Total'
      AND sex         = 'total'
      AND area_level  = 2
    GROUP BY year, area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja
)
SELECT *,
    ROUND(pop_65_plus * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14, 0), 1)                  AS aging_index,
    ROUND(pop_65_plus * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_15_64, 0), 1)                 AS old_age_dep,
    ROUND(pop_0_14 * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_15_64, 0), 1)                    AS youth_dep,
    ROUND((pop_0_14 + pop_65_plus) * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_15_64, 0), 1)    AS total_dep,
    ROUND(pop_15_64 * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_total, 0), 1)                   AS working_age_share
FROM age_buckets
ORDER BY year, area_estat
Calculation Note

Growth Rate

Annualized Growth Formula

rate = (pop_b / pop_a) ^ (1 / (year_b - year_a)) - 1

Census intervals are nominally 5-year steps (1920–2020) but are not uniform — the year gap ranges from 2 to 5 depending on historical disruptions. Never hardcode a 5-year denominator. Always compute the actual year gap from the data. The year_gap column in v_map_metrics provides this per row.

Query Cookbook

Canonical SQL for each panel and data surface in the dashboard. Copy-paste ready. Placeholder tokens in {braces} are Python f-string substitution points.

Map Queries always via v_map_metrics — never v_census
Population + Aging Metrics人口・高齢化指数 v_map_metrics
Pre-aggregated with period deltas. pop_delta and aging_index_delta are NULL for 1920 (no prior year).
SELECT area_estat, population, aging_index,
       pop_delta, aging_index_delta, prev_year, year_gap
FROM v_map_metrics
WHERE year = {year}
TFR Choropleth合計特殊出生率 v_map_metrics NULL pre-1960
Coverage begins 1960. Map metric selector snaps minimum year to 1960 when TFR is active. Do not infer zero for missing years.
SELECT area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja, year, tfr
FROM v_map_metrics
WHERE year = {year}
  AND tfr IS NOT NULL
Net Migration転入超過数 v_map_metrics NULL outside 1985–2020
Cumulative 5-year window ending on census year. 1985 is partial (4 years; source data begins 1982). Negative = net outflow.
SELECT area_estat, prefecture_name, prefecture_name_ja, year, net_migration
FROM v_map_metrics
WHERE year = {year}
  AND net_migration IS NOT NULL
Population Pyramid collapses 80+ bands into a canonical 80+ terminal
National Pyramid全国人口ピラミッド v_census
The inner CASE collapses all bands with age_start ≥ 80 into a single 80+ band for visual consistency across years with differing terminal bands.
SELECT age_group, age_start, sex, SUM(population) AS population
FROM (
    SELECT
        CASE WHEN age_start >= 80 THEN '80+' ELSE age_group END AS age_group,
        CASE WHEN age_start >= 80 THEN 80    ELSE age_start END AS age_start,
        sex, population
    FROM v_census
    WHERE year       = {year}
      AND age_scheme = 'scheme_a'
      AND age_group != 'Total'
      AND sex       != 'total'
      AND area_level = 2
)
GROUP BY age_group, age_start, sex
ORDER BY age_start
Single Prefecture Pyramid都道府県別人口ピラミッド v_census

Time Series all census years; swap area filter for prefecture overlay
National Population総人口 v_census
Uses the pre-stored Total row — the only valid use of age_group = 'Total'. Avoids the unknown-age double-count issue because Total is sourced directly.
SELECT year, SUM(population) AS total_pop
FROM v_census
WHERE age_group = 'Total'
  AND sex = 'total'

Aging Index Over Time高齢化指数推移 v_census
Swap area_level = 2 for area_estat = '{area_estat}' to render a prefecture overlay as a dashed line.
WITH age_buckets AS (
    SELECT year,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 65                               THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_65_plus,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start <= 10 AND age_end <= 14 THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_0_14
    FROM v_census
    WHERE age_scheme = 'scheme_a'
      AND age_group != 'Total'
      AND sex        = 'total'
      AND area_level = 2   -- swap for: AND area_estat = '{area_estat}'
    GROUP BY year
)
SELECT year,
    ROUND(pop_65_plus * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14, 0), 1) AS aging_index
FROM age_buckets
ORDER BY year
Population Share (三区分)年少・生産・老年人口割合 v_census
Denominator = sum of three buckets, not the Total row — intentionally excludes ~1.45M persons with unknown age. The three shares always sum to 100%.
WITH age_buckets AS (
    SELECT year,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start <= 10 AND age_end <= 14 THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_0_14,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 15 AND age_end <= 64 THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_15_64,
        SUM(CASE WHEN age_start >= 65                   THEN population ELSE 0 END) AS pop_65_plus
    FROM v_census
    WHERE age_scheme = 'scheme_a'
      AND age_group != 'Total'
      AND sex        = 'total'
      AND area_level = 2   -- swap for: AND area_estat = '{area_estat}'
    GROUP BY year
)
SELECT year,
    ROUND(pop_0_14    * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS youth_share,
    ROUND(pop_15_64   * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS working_share,
    ROUND(pop_65_plus * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS old_share,
    pop_0_14, pop_15_64, pop_65_plus
FROM age_buckets
ORDER BY year
TFR — National Average合計特殊出生率（全国平均） f_tfr no data pre-1960
National average = mean of all prefecture TFR values per year. Coverage begins 1960. Do not infer zero for pre-1960 — no rows exist.
-- National average TFR by year
SELECT year, ROUND(AVG(tfr), 2) AS tfr
FROM f_tfr
GROUP BY year
ORDER BY year

IPSS Projections bolted on at 2020; solid → dashed handoff
Handoff at 2020: Census observation lines end at 2020 (IPSS_HANDOFF_YEAR). IPSS projection lines begin at 2020 using a confirmed census observation as the anchor. The 2015 join point is avoided because IPSS upward-adjusts the 2015 baseline by ~1–2% (undercounting), producing a visible step discontinuity. Joining at 2020 eliminates this.
National Population Projection全国人口推計（三区分） f_national_projections
Fetch all three variants to build the high/low confidence band and medium continuation line. Variants differ only in fertility assumption (TFR trajectory); mortality held at medium in all three.
SELECT projection_year, variant, total_population
FROM f_national_projections
WHERE projection_year >= {IPSS_HANDOFF_YEAR}
ORDER BY projection_year, variant
Projected Population Shares将来人口割合推計 f_national_projections
Denominator matches the census share denominator — sum of three buckets — ensuring a seamless visual handoff between census and projection lines.
SELECT
    projection_year, variant,
    ROUND(pop_0_14    * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS youth_share,
    ROUND(pop_15_64   * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS working_share,
    ROUND(pop_65_plus * 100.0 / NULLIF(pop_0_14 + pop_15_64 + pop_65_plus, 0), 1) AS old_share
FROM f_national_projections
WHERE projection_year >= {IPSS_HANDOFF_YEAR}
ORDER BY projection_year, variant
